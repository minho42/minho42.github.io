# Privacy Policy

Gym Log does not collect any personal data.

Everything you enter stays on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, your
data syncs through your own private iCloud account so you can use it across your
devices — the developer has no access to it.

There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

In‑app purchases are handled by Apple; the developer does not receive your
payment information.
