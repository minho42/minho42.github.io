# Privacy Policy

Shift Planner doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no analytics, no
ads, no cookies, and no tracking.

**Your data:** your table settings (bed numbers, task names, header and footer)
are saved only in your own browser's local storage, so the page remembers them
next time. They are never sent anywhere, and the developer can't see them.

**Patient details:** the app is meant to be printed and filled in by hand.
Don't type patient information into it.

**Hosting:** the page is served by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, may
keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the site.

**Deleting your data:** clear this site's data in your browser settings.
**Reset** in the app puts the table back to the default.
