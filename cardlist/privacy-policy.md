# Privacy Policy

Card List is a loyalty card wallet. It does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Your cards

The cards you add are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they are synced to your devices through your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple. The developer cannot see or access them.

## Camera and photos

- **Camera** is used only to read a card's barcode. Nothing is recorded or saved.
- **Photos** you choose to import are read on your device to find barcodes and store names. The images are not saved or uploaded.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics or tracking
- No ads
- No data sold or shared with anyone

## Deleting your data

Delete a card in the app, or delete the app to remove everything on the device. Cards synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.
