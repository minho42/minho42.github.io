# Privacy Policy

Voice Support doesn't collect any data. There's no server, no account, no
analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

**Your data:** everything in the app — voice messages, names, titles, and your
history of sessions and check-ins — is stored only on your iPhone. The
developer can't see or access it.

**Microphone:** used only when you choose to record a message.

**Speech recognition:** used to suggest a title from a message's first few
words. This happens on your iPhone, and audio is never sent anywhere.

**Sharing:** your data only leaves your iPhone when you choose to send it — for
example, by sharing a message or sending someone a request for one. It goes
only where you send it.

**Purchases:** handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details.

**Deleting your data:** delete messages or clear your history in the app at any
time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.
