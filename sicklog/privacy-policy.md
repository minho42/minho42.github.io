# Privacy Policy

Sick Log doesn't collect any data. There's no server, no account, no analytics,
no ads, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.

**Your data:** everything you enter — people's details and photos,
illnesses, symptoms, temperatures, medications, doctor visits, and notes — is
stored on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your own private
iCloud account through Apple. The developer can't see or access it. You can
turn off iCloud for the app in iOS Settings.

**Sharing:** apart from syncing with your own iCloud, your data only leaves your
device when you choose to send it — for example, by exporting a file or sharing
with family. It goes only where you send it.

**Sharing with family:** if you share your logs, the people you invite can see
and change everything in them, including people's details and photos. Shared
logs stay in the iCloud account of whoever shared them and sync to the people
invited through Apple's iCloud. The developer still can't see them. You can
remove someone or stop sharing at any time in Settings → Manage Sharing.

**Children:** the app is often used to record information about children. Since
the developer never receives any data, that information stays with you.

**Purchases:** handled by Apple. The app never sees your payment details.

**Deleting your data:** delete anything in the app at any time. Deleting the app
removes its data from your device, and you can remove the iCloud copy in iOS
Settings → your name → iCloud.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the update will be posted here.
