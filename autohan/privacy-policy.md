# AutoHan Privacy Policy

AutoHan does not collect or share any personal data, and it doesn't send anything off your Mac.

This policy covers AutoHan itself. What you type still goes to the app you're typing in, and that app has its own privacy policy.

- **What you type.** As an input method, AutoHan sees every key you press while it's selected, so it can work out which language you're typing. While it runs, it holds the word you're typing, the last word and your corrections in memory. It never writes your keys to a log, and it never saves them unless you turn on *Remember my corrections*.
- **No internet.** The app has no network access. macOS enforces this: AutoHan runs in the App Sandbox without network permission, which blocks it from making connections.
- **No tracking.** There are no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party services.
- **Passwords.** When macOS secure input is on, as it is in standard password fields, AutoHan passes your keys straight to the app. Some prompts, such as some terminal password prompts, don't use secure input. AutoHan treats those like any other typing: it handles the keys in memory and never logs or saves them unless you correct the word with *Remember my corrections* on.
- **Corrections.** *Remember my corrections* is off by default, and until you turn it on, corrections are kept in memory only. When you turn it on, AutoHan saves the words you've corrected since it started, and any you correct later, on your Mac. They aren't sent anywhere, but a backup of your Mac may include them. Turning the setting off stops saving but keeps the saved words, and so does deleting the app. To delete them, choose **Clear All** in AutoHan Settings while the setting is on.
- **Settings.** Your preferences, including the language you set for each app, are stored on your Mac.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
