# Kindly Words

Study the words you look up on your Kindle, in the sentences you first read
them in.

## Features

- Imports your Kindle's Vocabulary Builder and highlights with the free Mac app
  over USB, or from files on iPhone.
- Spaced-repetition study with typed recall, listening, and matching.
- Home and lock screen widget.
- Private iCloud sync between your Mac and iPhone.

Free for your first 30 words. A one-time purchase unlocks your whole library.

## Language

English only. The dictionary and pronunciation are English, so words you look
up in Kindle books in other languages come in without a dictionary entry.

## A more natural voice

Words and definitions are read aloud with the best English voice on your
iPhone. For a much more natural one, download a Premium or Enhanced voice in
Settings → Accessibility → Read & Speak → Voices → English. Kindly Words uses
it automatically.

## Privacy

Your words, sentences, and highlights stay on your devices and in your own
iCloud. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
