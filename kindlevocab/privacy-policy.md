# Privacy Policy

Kindly Words does not collect any personal data.

Your vocabulary, the sentences you read them in, and your highlights stay on your
devices. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync through your own private
iCloud account so you can use them on your Mac and iPhone. The developer has no
access to them.

On iPhone, if you tap "Fetch missing definitions", those words are looked up at
[dictionaryapi.dev](https://dictionaryapi.dev). Only the word itself is sent,
never your sentences, highlights, or book titles. The Mac app uses the built-in
macOS dictionary.

There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your
payment information.
