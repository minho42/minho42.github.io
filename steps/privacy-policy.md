# Privacy Policy

Shift Steps doesn't collect any data. There's no server of its own, no account,
no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

**Apple Health:** the app reads your step count to show the steps during each
shift. It never writes to Health, and your Health data is never used for
advertising or shared, except for the Legends totals below if you join.

**Calendar:** if you choose a roster calendar, the app reads your shifts from
that calendar only. It never changes your calendars or reads any others.

**Your data:** your shifts and their step counts are stored on your device
only. They aren't synced to iCloud, and the developer can't see or access them.

**Legends (optional):** Legends is off unless you join. If you do, these are
saved to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) public database so others can see the
rankings:

- a random ID the app creates, which doesn't identify you
- your country
- a nickname and workplace, only if you enter them
- for each month and all time: your total steps, number of shifts, average
  steps per shift, and steps per hour

Your shift times, dates, and other Health data are never uploaded. Leaving
Legends deletes your entries.

**Sharing:** a shift card only leaves your device when you share it, and goes
only where you send it.

**Deleting your data:** delete shifts in the app at any time, or delete the app
to remove everything from your device. Leave Legends first to delete your
entries there.
