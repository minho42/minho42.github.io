# Shift Steps

An iPhone app for shift workers that counts your steps **during your shifts
only**, not the whole day, so you can see how much each shift really took.

## Features

- **Calendar** — your shifts at a glance, with the steps for each one. Add
  them yourself, or read them from your roster calendar.
- **Day panel** — tap a day to see its steps, live while the shift is on, and
  how they compare with your usual shift of that type.
- **Stats** — steps per hour, total steps, and averages by shift type, by
  month, year, or all time.
- **Legends** — an optional ranking of the toughest shifts in your country, by
  steps per hour. Off until you join.
- **Share** — a card of any shift's steps, to send to colleagues or post.

Steps come from Apple Health, so an iPhone or Apple Watch counts them for you.

## Privacy

Your shifts and steps stay on your device. No account, no analytics, no ads.
Legends shares only your shift totals, and only if you join. See the
[privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
