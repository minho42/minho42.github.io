# Privacy Policy

Camtionary does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or
tracking, and the developer receives nothing about you or what you read.

## The camera

Camtionary uses the camera to read the words in front of you. The text is read on your
iPhone, and the camera's picture is never saved or sent anywhere. A photo you take of a
book's cover is kept on your iPhone only, to show next to that book's words.

## Looking up words

The dictionary is built into the app and works offline. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence,
the meaning that fits the sentence is chosen on your iPhone, by Apple's on-device model.
The sentence isn't sent anywhere.

## Wikipedia and Wiktionary

When you look up a word, Camtionary sends that word, and nothing else, to Wikipedia and
Wiktionary, run by the Wikimedia Foundation, to show what they say about it. To find
Wikipedia's article for the meaning you're reading, it also sends that meaning's dictionary
number (its WordNet ID) to Wikidata, also run by Wikimedia. It never sends the camera's picture,
the sentence around the word, or anything about you. How Wikimedia handles requests:
[Wikimedia's privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

You can turn this off in Settings → Wikipedia & Wiktionary. Then nothing leaves your iPhone
when you look up a word.

Pages from Wikipedia, Wiktionary or Google open only when you tap a button for them. They
open in Safari, where those sites' own privacy policies apply.

## On your device

The words you look up, the sentences you found them in, your books and topics, their cover
photos and your settings are saved on your iPhone only. They're deleted when you delete
the app.

## Purchases

Camtionary Pro is bought through Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.
