# Privacy Policy

Parking Defense has no accounts, and the developer does not collect or receive any
personal data.

## Ads

The game is free and shows full-screen ads from **Google AdMob**: never in your first 5
games, and then one after a lost game. To show and measure these ads, Google's ads
software in the game may collect:

- your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks),
- an approximate location derived from your IP address,
- how you interact with the ads, and
- crash and performance diagnostics.

This data goes to Google, not to the developer. If you are in the European Economic
Area or the UK, the game first asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You
can refuse tracking (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) and still play; you will
then see less personalised ads. How Google uses this data:
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## On your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so they are there next time: the
ranger look you picked, your best wave, the most cars you have booked in one game, how
many games you have played and whether an ad is still due. They never leave your device
and are deleted when you delete the app.

The game never uses your camera, microphone or photos.
