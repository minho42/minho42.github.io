# Privacy Policy

Parking Defense has no accounts, and the developer does not collect or receive any
personal data.

## Ads

The game is free and shows full-screen ads from **Google AdMob**. To show and measure these
ads, Google's ads software in the game may collect:

- your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking when iOS asks),
- an approximate location derived from your IP address,
- how you interact with the ads, and
- crash and performance diagnostics.

This data goes to Google, not to the developer. If you are in the European Economic Area or
the UK, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can refuse tracking
(Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) and still play; you will then see less
personalised ads. How Google uses this data:
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## On your device

The game saves a few things on your own device so they are there next time: your settings,
your best results, how many games you have played and whether an ad is still due. They never
leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

The game never uses your camera, microphone or photos.
