# Privacy Policy

Parking Defense does not collect any personal data.

The game works fully offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking, and it never uses your camera, microphone or photos.

The game saves a few things on your own device so they are there next time: the
ranger look you picked, your best wave and the most cars you have booked in one game.
They never leave your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

If a future version adds anything online (such as leaderboards), this policy will be
updated before that version is released.
