# Privacy Policy

Kindly Words does not collect any personal data. No ads, no analytics, no
tracking.

Your words, sentences, and highlights stay on your devices and sync through
your own private iCloud account. The developer has no access to them.

To show book covers, the app downloads them from Amazon using each book's
Amazon ID. Nothing else is sent.

Purchases are handled by Apple. The developer never sees your payment details.
