# Privacy Policy

Gimbap is designed to respect your privacy.

## What we collect

Only what's needed for a licence, described under [Licence](#licence). Apart from that, Gimbap
does **not** collect, store, transmit or share any personal information, and it has no analytics
or tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your Mac

To work like the Dock, Gimbap reads things on your Mac, and only on your Mac:

- your Dock's apps, folders and settings,
- which apps are running and their open windows (with **Accessibility** permission),
- what's in the Trash, and each app's recent documents (with **Full Disk Access**).

This is used only to show your dock and its menus. It is **never** sent to us or to anyone else.

## Licence

When you activate a licence, Gimbap sends your licence key and your Mac's name to our licence
server, which runs on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com) and stores them. The name is there
so a licence can be told apart on each Mac it's active on, and so a Mac that's set up again keeps
its place. About once a month Gimbap checks the licence is still valid, sending the key and its
activation ID. Nothing else is sent. Like any website, Cloudflare may log the request's IP address
under [its privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

Deactivating a Mac in Dock Settings ▸ Licence deletes its name from the server.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Stripe and its Link service, which sell Gimbap on our behalf, under
[Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). We never see your card details. We can see
a record of your order in Stripe, including your email address, which we use only to help with
your purchase. The licence server stores your licence key with a reference to that order, so a
lost key can be found and a refund can end it.

## Updates

Once a day, Gimbap downloads a small list of available versions from this website (hosted on
GitHub Pages) to see if there's an update. No information about you or your Mac is included in
that request. Like any website, GitHub may log the request's IP address under
[its privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
You can turn update checks off in Dock Settings ▸ Updates.
